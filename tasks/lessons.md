### The hero is intentionally subtle; the copy is fixed
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Trigger:** The user rejected a rewritten hero headline and CTA: "the hero was intentionally subtle and understated".
- **Rule:** The hero shows only the mark, the "WebTree / Global" wordmark and the tagline "Strategic Capital · Systematic Execution". Keep all approved copy word for word: section labels About, Philosophy and Contact; nav links About and Contact; the About sentence, pillars, principles and contact items; the meta title and descriptions. Propose copy changes separately; do not ship them with design work.
- **Why:** The site is deliberately opaque and understated for its audience of partners and co-investors.

### Label the tree with the pillars, not new words
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Trigger:** The user removed the proposed region words ("Roots", "Branches", "Canopy") and asked for "03 — Ventures" instead.
- **Rule:** Label tree parts and rows with the existing pillar number and title ("01 — Capital", "02 — Technology", "03 — Ventures"). Do not add explanatory words to the page unless the user approves them.
- **Why:** The pillar names already explain the mapping; new terms are unapproved copy.

### Scroll effects need a mobile version
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Trigger:** The user asked why mobile did not have the tree highlight effect; it was desktop-only.
- **Rule:** Design each scroll interaction for small screens too (here: a pinned band under the header with the tree and labels). Drop only the part that cannot work (the hero-to-stage glide crosses the wordmark on narrow screens) and say so.
- **Why:** Many visitors arrive on phones; a desktop-only signature effect is invisible to them.
