### The hero is intentionally subtle; the copy is fixed
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Trigger:** The user rejected a rewritten hero headline and CTA: "the hero was intentionally subtle and understated".
- **Rule:** The hero shows only the mark, the "WebTree / Global" wordmark and the tagline "Strategic Capital · Systematic Execution". - **Why:** The site is deliberately opaque and understated for its audience of partners and co-investors.
