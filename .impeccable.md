## Design Context

### Users
Prospective partners and co-investors. They arrive from an introduction, an
email signature or a search, usually before a first meeting. Their job is to
decide quickly whether WebTree Global is a credible, real and serious
counterparty, and to find how to make contact.

### Brand Personality
Patient, exacting, discreet. The site states what the company is in plain
words, shows restraint, and does not oversell. It must feel like the brass
plate and annual report of a private holding company, not a startup or a
crypto fund.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark "vault": warm near-black ground, ivory type, champagne gold from the
  logo as the only accent. No teal, no secondary accent colour.
- The hex-tree logo is the only illustration. It is drawn as exact vector
  geometry (`lib/tree-geometry.ts`) so it can grow in the hero and highlight
  one region per focus area: roots (Capital), branches (Technology), canopy
  (Ventures).
- Type: Spectral (light serif) for statements and titles; Archivo for body
  text and wide-tracked capital labels.
- Structure comes from a 12-column grid and 1px hairline rules, like a
  ledger. No cards, glass panels, glows, gradient text, orbs or noise.
- Anti-references: luxury templates with centred wordmarks and watermark
  logos; "AI" palettes (cyan on dark, neon, purple gradients); glowing data
  lines.

### Design Principles
1. Say what the company is above the fold, in one sentence.
2. Readable first: all text meets WCAG AA contrast; no 10px grey labels.
3. One accent, used rarely. Gold marks the logo, links and one emphasised
   phrase per section.
4. Rhythm through layout change: hero split, ledger rows, columns, contact
   block. Do not repeat the same component down the page.
5. Motion only on entrance, never looping, and none when the visitor
   prefers reduced motion. The page works with JavaScript off.

### Content rule
Stay opaque. Use only the approved copy (pillars, principles, contact
details). Restructure it, but do not add claims, figures or company names.
